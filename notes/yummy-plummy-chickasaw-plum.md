## Ingredients

### Simple Measures

- [ ] 7g Mint leaves
- [ ] 1/4 cup Lime juice (25g)
- [ ] 1 cup Water (60g)
- [ ] 1/4 cup Maple syrup (12g sugar)
- [ ] 1 cup Sugar (320g sugar)
- [ ] 500ml Chickasaw plum concentrate (88.77g sugar)
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Cardamom

### Metric Measures

- [ ] 7g Mint leaves
- [ ] 25g Lime juice
- [ ] 60g Water
- [ ] 12g Maple syrup (12g sugar)
- [ ] 320g Sugar (320g sugar)
- [ ] 500ml Chickasaw plum concentrate (88.77g sugar)
- [ ] 0.6g Cardamom

#### Directions

Blend:

- Mint leaves
- Lime juice
- Water

Bring to boil then strain.
Add to Boil:

- Maple syrup
- Sugar

Boil until sugar dissolves.
Add Chickasaw plum concentrate, cardamom and water/tea/juice to reach a total volume of 2000ml.
Blend lightly to mix.
