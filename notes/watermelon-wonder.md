## Ingredients

### Simple Measures

- [ ] 1750 ml Watermelon juice (1.5g sugar / 29.57 ml)
- [ ] 150 ml Lemon juice
- [ ] 100 ml Lime juice
- [ ] 7g Mint leaves
- [ ] 1 cup sugar (200g sugar)

### Metric Measures

- [ ] 1750 ml Watermelon juice (1.5g sugar / 29.57 ml)
- [ ] 150 ml Lemon juice
- [ ] 100 ml Lime juice
- [ ] 7g Mint leaves
- [ ] 200g sugar (200g sugar)

#### Directions

Blend:

- 150 ml Lemon juice
- 100 ml Lime juice
- 7g Mint leaves
- 1 cup Watermelon Juice

Bring to boil then strain.
Add to Boil:

- 1 cup sugar (200 sugar)

Boil until sugar dissolves.
Add watermelon juice to 2000ml.
Blend lightly to mix.
