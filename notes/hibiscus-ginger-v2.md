## Ingredients
### Simple Measures

- [ ] 900ml Hibiscus Tea
- [ ] 100 ml lemon juice
- [ ] 7g mint leaves
- [ ] 4oz ginger
- [ ] 0.5 tsp cardamon
- [ ] 0.5 cup sugar (100g)
- [ ] 1/8 tsp Citric Acid
- [ ] 1/4 cup water
- [ ] 4 drops Vanilla

### Metric Measures

- [ ] 900ml Hibiscus Tea
- [ ] 100 ml lemon juice
- [ ] 7 g mint leaves
- [ ] 114g ginger (approximately 4oz)
- [ ] 2.5g cardamon
- [ ] 100g sugar
- [ ] 60 ml water
- [ ] 0.5g Citric Acid (approximately 1/8 tsp)
- [ ] 0.2ml Vanilla (approximately 4 drops)



#### Directions

Blend:

- Add 100 ml lemon juice, 1/4 cup water, 7 g of mint leaves, 4 oz of ginger to the blender.
- Blend thoroughly.
Bring to boil then strain.
Add to Boil:

- Sugar
- Cardamon 
- Citric Acid
- Vanilla

Boil until sugar dissolves.
Add water/tea/juice to reach a total volume of 2000ml.
Blend lightly to mix.
