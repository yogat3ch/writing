Hibiscus Ginger Mint Lemonade

## Ingredients

### Simple Measures

- [ ] 450ml Hibiscus Concentrate (12.2g sugar + 16.8g added / 360ml)
- [ ] 100 ml lemon juice
- [ ] 7g mint leaves
- [ ] 3oz ginger
- [ ] 0.5 tsp cardamon
- [ ] 1 cup sugar (200g)
- [ ] 1/4 cup water

### Metric Measures

- [ ] 450ml Hibiscus Concentrate (12.2g sugar + 16.8g added / 360ml)
- [ ] 100 ml lemon juice
- [ ] 7 g mint leaves
- [ ] 85g ginger (approximately 3oz)
- [ ] 2.5g cardamon
- [ ] 200g sugar
- [ ] 60 ml water

#### Directions

Blend:

- Add 100 ml lemon juice, 1/4 cup water, 7 g of mint leaves, 3 oz. of ginger, and 0.5 tsp cardamon to the blender.
- Blend thoroughly.

Bring to boil then strain.

Add to Boil:

- Sugar

Boil until sugar dissolves.
Add water to 2000ml.
Blend lightly to mix.
