### Honeysickle Tea

- [ ] 80g Honeysuckle
- [ ] 4000ml Water
- [ ] Makes 3450ml Honeysuckle Tea

### **Ingredients:**

- [ ] 100ml or 1/2 cup honey (14g / 1tbsp sugar)
- [ ] 1 cup sugar (200g / 1 cup sugar)
- [ ] 70ml or 1/3 cup lemon juice
- [ ] 1/2 tsp citric acid
- [ ] 1800ml Honeysuckle Tea

Makes 2000ml

### Directions

Boil:

- [ ] 100ml or 1/2 cup honey (14g / 1tbsp sugar)
- [ ] 1 cup sugar (200g / 1 cup sugar)
- [ ] 70ml or 1/3 cup lemon juice
- [ ] 1/2 tsp citric acid
- [ ] 1 cup Honeysuckle Tea

Until sugar and honey is dissolved.
