## Ingredients

### Simple Measures

- [ ] 12 lbs Chickasaw Plums (None)
- [ ] 6 oz Ginger (None)
- [ ] 6 oz Water (None)
- [ ] 3 lbs Sugar (3 lbs)
- [ ] 2 tbsp Vanilla (None)
- [ ] 3/4 cup Maple Syrup (0.75 cup)
- [ ] 1/2 cup Lime Juice (None)
- [ ] 1 tbsp Balsamic/Apple Cider Vinegar (None)

### Metric Measures

- [ ] 5.44 kg Chickasaw Plums (None)
- [ ] 170 g Ginger (None)
- [ ] 170 g Water (None)
- [ ] 1.36 kg Sugar (1.36 kg)
- [ ] 30 ml Vanilla (None)
- [ ] 177 ml Maple Syrup (0.75 cup)
- [ ] 118 ml Lime Juice (None)
- [ ] 15 ml Balsamic/Apple Cider Vinegar (None)

#### Directions

Blend:

- Heat fruit on medium heat until thawed and stirrable.
- Once thawed, squeeze out and pour off excess juice for popsicles, leaving 1/5 the total strained juice.
- Blend remaining fruit (Ensure no seeds).
- Stir in: 1 parts sugar to 3 parts pulp, (1/4 the total volume/weight)

Bring to boil then strain.
Add to Boil:

- Add Ginger boil mixture, vanilla, maple syrup, lime juice, and vinegar.

Boil until ginger, vanilla, maple syrup, lime juice, and vinegar dissolves.
Blend lightly to mix.
